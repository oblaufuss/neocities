John Meets John

When he was eight, Linnell (born in New York) moved to Lincoln, MA, where Flansburgh lived. Attending the same schools, they bumped into each other in a number of ways:

  • Flansburgh, along with everyone at their middle school, was told to write Linnell a get-well-soon card despite not yet knowing him. Linnell was just as baffled to recieve the cards. (Gigantic clip, 2003)
  • Linnell liked drawing cartoons
JF: Well, you know, John is a year older than me. So it doesn't seem like that big a deal when you're, you know, 41 and 42, to be a year different, but in a lot of ways our relationship is still kind of frozen in that way. That he's always a year older than me.
--Gigantic

Don't Worry Kyoko

JF: We had a really good friend, this guy Jimmy Mack, who turned us on to a lot of what essentially was proto-punk music. He played us The Ramones and he played us Iggy and the Stooges and all this stuff that in 1976, 77, was pretty much a local New York experience.
JL: You know I think at that time I was geared up to be a real elitist snob about music, but the thing about Jimmy was that he was really into the most mass-market pop stuff as well.
--Gigantic

Jamie Lincoln Kitman

  • He was trained as a lawyer, and also writes for car magazines, including Car Talk.
  • He wrote the Our Roadies, Ourselves article, source of many an insane anecdote about life on the road for the '80s Giants. (scan) (text-only)
    • Bill Krauss later confirmed that he did really have a gun pulled on him by a man called Johnny Banana, but John Flansburgh swears nobody ever called Linnell "The Professor" or himself "Jack".
    • The name of this article is a play on classic feminist book Our Bodies, Ourselves

Non-Architectural Fashion

John Flansburgh:

  • Wore purple nail polish sometimes in the 80s. (He hasn't said why he stopped, but getting tired of speculation on his sexuality might have had something to do with it, so don't be weird about this one.) Now, he wears clear polish to protect his fingers while strumming; silver polish showed up on his Sapphire Bullets persona.
  • "I remember doing an interview with somebody, some tabloid press, where it seemed like the only topic of the interview was that they were trying to figure out in sly ways whether or not John and I were gay... which was like,"(uncomfortable)"Okay. And the thing is, I'm from New York City, I have many gay friends, I'm a gay-positive person. I'm not going to make any homophobic statements or declare anything strange, so it was an awkward conversation to have..." (joking)"and is that because you're gay, John?" (sarcastic, resigned)"It's because I'm gay." (excerpt)
  • Frequently wore TMBG t-shirts in the 80s and 90s
  • Infamously wore a Northern Scum t-shirt which was obscured by his guitar strap to read "RTHERN CUM"
  • Wore the bright orange lining to a jacket on TV, which the washed-out tape colors converted to hot pink.
  • Loves cardigans; the most striking probably being the one that's patterned after the carpet from The Shining.
  • Has a WOMAN POWER pin
  • Uses a guitar strap with an owl pattern.
